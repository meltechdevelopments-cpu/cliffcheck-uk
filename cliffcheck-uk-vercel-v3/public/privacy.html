Privacy Policy & Data Notice

Last updated: November 2026 · CliffCheck UK (cliffcheck-uk.vercel.app)

1. What CliffCheck UK Does

CliffCheck UK helps used-car shoppers check scheduled manufacturer and UK specialist maintenance intervals (such as wet timing belts, timing chains, gearbox services, DPF usage patterns, and UK road-tax thresholds) via our web application and Mozilla Firefox browser add-on.

2. Browser Add-On Data Privacy (Zero Tracking)

Our Mozilla Firefox browser add-on analyses vehicle specifications (make, model, year, engine size, fuel type, gearbox, and mileage) locally inside your browser on supported UK car-listing pages (AutoTrader UK, eBay Motors, and CarGurus UK).

  • We do not collect, store, or transmit your browsing history, personal identity, cookies, or search activity.
  • Your free-check counter and optional 30-Day Pass status are saved locally in your own browser storage (storage.local / localStorage).
  • If you choose to purchase and activate a 30-Day Pass, your Pass license key is sent solely to our payment and licensing partner Polar.sh (api.polar.sh) to verify that the Pass is valid.

3. UK Registration Lookups

When you enter a UK vehicle registration mark on our website, it is used solely to retrieve public vehicle technical and MOT history data via official UK government APIs (Open Government Licence v3.0). We do not build personal profiles from registration searches.

4. Payments & Partner Links

Payments for the optional £9.99 30-Day Pass are processed securely by Polar Software Inc. (Polar.sh) and Stripe; CliffCheck UK never sees or stores your payment card details. Our reports also include optional referral links to third-party vehicle history providers (such as carVertical / MotorCheck) for stolen, finance, and write-off checks; if you purchase a history report through those links, we may earn an affiliate commission at no extra cost to you.

5. Maintenance Guidance Disclaimer

CliffCheck UK surfaces general manufacturer and UK independent-specialist schedule intervals based on vehicle specification, age, and mileage. Because digital MOT records do not record private garage invoices, always ask the seller for itemised VAT receipts confirming whether scheduled maintenance has already been completed.
